## Leakage and shortcut checks
- Exact content overlap between train and test: 0
- A large majority of True.csv articles contain a Reuters dateline such as 'WASHINGTON (Reuters) -', while Fake.csv almost never does. A bag-of-words model can exploit this publishing artefact.
- Real articles in this dataset use subjects politicsNews and worldnews; fake articles use a disjoint subject set. Subject is therefore a near-perfect source shortcut and is not used as a model feature.

## Limitations
- The ISOT-style Fake/True split is known to mix source style with the label.
- High in-domain accuracy does not imply real-world fact checking.
- Independent publisher-held-out news was not available in this repository.
